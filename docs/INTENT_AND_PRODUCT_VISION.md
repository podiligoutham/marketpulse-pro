# Intent and Product Vision

**Working name:** MarketPulse Pro

**Mission:** Learn to design, implement, test, operate, and evolve a credible market-analysis application, end to end, while developing senior-level React and Java expertise.

## Target users
An individual investor or learner who wants to explore market moves, technical indicators, catalysts, and paper-trading ideas with clear evidence and caveats.

## Product pillars
1. **Discover:** liquid stocks, unusual relative volume, market movers, sectors, and catalysts.
2. **Understand:** price/volume charts, transparent indicators, explanations, and time-stamped sources.
3. **Evaluate:** bullish/bearish technical confluence with reproducible calculations.
4. **Practice:** simulated order entry, risk sizing, positions, journal, and backtests.
5. **Learn:** explainability, educational tooltips, post-trade review, and engineering craftsmanship.

## Design principles
Fast, readable, responsive, accessible, trustworthy. Inspired by polished brokerage UX but with original visual design. Clearly distinguish historical data, delayed quotes, and live licensed data. Show data freshness and unavailable indicators explicitly.

## Success criteria
- Reliable documented local setup and CI.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible frontend with robust loading/error/empty states.
- Correct and tested indicator implementations against independent fixtures.
- Backend modules with clear contracts, observability, migrations, and authentication where appropriate.
- Reproducible paper-trading accounting and backtesting without look-ahead bias.
- AWS deployment with documented cost/security posture.
- Developer can explain architecture and tradeoffs in a senior-engineer interview.

## Ethical boundary
Educational analysis only. No claim of predictive certainty. Confluence scores describe indicator agreement, never win probability.
